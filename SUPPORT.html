Common questions

Is my data on a server somewhere?

No. Everything you log lives only on your iPhone. The app sends no analytics, no telemetry, no error reports. Your data leaves the device only when you choose to share a report (PDF) using your phone's share sheet.

How do I delete my data?

Settings, then Data and Privacy, then "Delete all my logs" removes everything you have logged. "Delete everything" also removes your profile and preferences. Deleting the app from your phone also removes all data, since nothing is stored elsewhere.

Is the app a medical device or a diagnosis?

No. The app is informational. It records what you log and presents the patterns. Anything in the report is something to discuss with your healthcare provider if you want clinical confirmation.

What is the MRS?

The Menopause Rating Scale, an eleven-factor instrument used by clinicians to characterize the menopausal transition. The app uses the published wordings.
