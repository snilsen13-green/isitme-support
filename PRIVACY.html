Privacy Policy

Effective: 2026-05-06

What this app does with your data

Everything you log in Is It Me or Is It Meno? stays on your iPhone. The app does not send your data to any server, to us, or to anyone else. There is no account, no sign in, no password.

What data is collected

Only the data you log yourself: daily check-in ratings, hot flash and other event entries, cycle days, body-map pain entries, daily quality ratings, free-text notes, and the preferences you set (your name if you provide one, language, personality mode, tracking preferences, notification times).

The app does not collect:

  • Your name unless you choose to enter it.
  • Your location.
  • Any contact information.
  • Browsing or usage analytics.
  • Crash reports.
  • Device identifiers for advertising.

Where the data is stored

Locally on your iPhone, in an app-private SQLite database that only Is It Me? can read. The data is included in your iPhone's standard backups (iCloud Backup or computer backup) the same way other app data is, unless you have disabled app backups for this app in iOS Settings.

What gets sent off the device

Nothing, unless you explicitly share a generated report. When you tap Share on a PDF report, iOS opens the standard share sheet and you choose where the report goes (email, Messages, Files, your healthcare provider's portal, etc.). At that point the PDF leaves the device because you sent it. The app itself never transmits any data on its own.

Notifications

The app schedules local reminders for your daily check-in and sleep check-in. These are local notifications generated and delivered by iOS on your device. They are not push notifications and they do not pass through any server.

Third parties

The app does not use any third-party analytics, advertising, or crash reporting services. There are no SDKs that share your data with anyone.

This version does not read from or write to Apple Health (HealthKit). A future version may offer optional integration to read cycle data already on your device. If and when that ships, the app will request your explicit permission, the data will stay on your device, and this policy will be updated to describe what is read.

Children

This app is intended for adults experiencing the menopausal transition and is not directed at children. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 13.

Your rights

Because all your data is on your device, you control it directly:

  • Delete all logs. Settings then Data and Privacy then "Delete all my logs".
  • Delete everything. Settings then Data and Privacy then "Delete everything" removes your logs, profile, and preferences.
  • Export. Generate a PDF report and share or save it via the iOS share sheet.
  • Uninstall. Removing the app from your iPhone removes all of its data.

We do not have copies of your data, so we cannot return or delete data on your behalf. There is no server-side record of you or your activity.

Changes to this policy

Any change that broadens what the app does with your data will be communicated in the app update release notes. The effective date at the top of this document marks the most recent version.
